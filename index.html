Who I am

I’m Ryan Pascal. I have a business degree from UAB’s Collat School of Business and years in B2B sales and account management, across construction software, RFID, construction materials, high-end hospital printing systems, and steel distribution e-commerce. I’m trained in legal research, data analysis and technical writing.

I’m not a lawyer. Every decision is yours. I research the facts, put everything in writing, and make sure you’re heard.

What I do

  • Research and a written report

    I look up the bulletins, programs, complaint records and repair costs that apply to your car, and put them in a report with every source cited.

  • Emails to the dealer and manufacturer

    With your written authorization, I write to them on your behalf. You’re copied on every message, and you approve each one before it goes out.

  • Follow-ups and escalation

    When they go quiet, I follow up. When the dealer stalls, I take it to the manufacturer’s corporate office.

  • A complete written record

    Every email, document and date, kept in one place, so nothing gets lost and nothing gets denied later.

Always free.

I don’t charge for this, and I don’t accept tips or gifts. I’m selective about what I take on, and I see it through to the end.

How it works

  1. You sign a written authorization.

    It says I’m not your lawyer, I don’t give legal advice, you make every decision, and there’s no payment.

  2. Everything happens by email.

    No phone calls. You’re copied on every message, so you always see exactly what was said.

  3. You make every decision.

    I research and write. You decide what gets sent and what to do next.
