How the record is examined

  • Start with the source

    Published manufacturer programs, technical instructions, repair estimates, and written correspondence do different jobs. Each is read for what it actually establishes, not what someone hopes it might establish.

  • Test the specific criteria

    Some programs cover a defined condition rather than every failure of a component. The question is which condition was observed, what testing supports it, and which published criteria were applied.

  • Preserve the dealer’s findings

    Technician notes, diagnostic observations, test results, inspection records, and the repair order are checked for completeness. A missing record stays an unanswered question, not a guessed answer.

  • Keep two manufacturer questions separate

    Does the published coverage program apply? If not, is goodwill assistance available? The answers, reasons, and terms should be clear in writing. A customer-support tracking number is not a repair approval.

  • Make every cost and condition visible

    Before additional testing or repairs, the owner decides after reviewing the proposed work, possible charges, and any conditions. Written offers should distinguish covered work, owner costs, and relevant dates.

  • Keep the owner in control

    Every outgoing message is written by the owner, in the owner’s own words. The owner is visibly included in email, receives the responses, and makes the decisions.* Communication is by email only.

Three different responsibilities.

  1. The dealer documents

    What condition was observed, what tests were completed, and which specific requirement was or was not met?

  2. The manufacturer reviews

    Does the published program apply? If it does not, is separate goodwill assistance available, and on what written terms?

  3. The owner decides

    What work, if any, to approve; what costs to accept; and how to proceed after reading the actual written answers.

A documented diagnosis, a coverage decision, and an offer of assistance are not interchangeable. The record should make clear which of those exists, which is pending, and what evidence supports each. No result is promised.
