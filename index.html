Dealer Pushback Closed to new requests

Everything in writing.

Dealer Pushback is a personal volunteer project. It asks a dealer or manufacturer for a clear, written decision about a repair, and the owner makes every decision.

It is closed. It is not accepting requests, and it will not reopen.

Who I am

I’m Ryan Pascal. I have a business degree from UAB’s Collat School of Business. I do this unpaid, on my own time.

I’m not an attorney, and I don’t give legal advice. The owner makes every decision.

How it’s done

  • The owner’s request

    The request belongs to the owner. Every message is written by the owner, in her own words, and she is copied on every one.*

  • Facts, quoted exactly

    Statements are labeled as what the owner reports, what a document shows, or what research found. Published program terms are quoted word for word, with the source. If something is stated wrong, the owner corrects it.

  • Decisions in writing

    The request is for a written decision, the criteria behind it, and the records that already exist. Email only, never phone calls.

  • Nothing public

    No names, stories, dealers or details are ever posted anywhere. Nothing is shared beyond the people who need it to make a decision.

Always free.

No payment, tips, gifts or donations. No ads, sponsors or affiliate links. No referrals to law firms, repair shops or car buyers, and nothing accepted from them. No reviews, testimonials or success stories. Not now, and not later.

Closed to new requests.

Dealer Pushback is a personal volunteer project, not a business or profession. It took on requests only under extremely strict criteria. It has ended, and it will not reopen. Work already underway is being completed. There is no contact form here, and requests for help are not accepted.
